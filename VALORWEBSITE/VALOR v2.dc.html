Guide

Up and running on your phone

  1. Get an executor

    On a phone, Delta (Android or iPhone). On a PC, Potassium. Arceus X, Real, Madium, Volt and Wave work too.

  2. Copy

    Copy the loader from the script hub above. It’s one line for every game.

  3. Paste

    Open the game, open Delta, paste the line into its editor and tap Execute.

  4. Play

    The menu for that game opens. No key for your first 3 days, then a free one from our Discord.

WORKS ON  Delta · Potassium · Arceus X · Real · Madium · Volt · Wave

PARTLY  Xeno · Solara — they lack functions some games need
