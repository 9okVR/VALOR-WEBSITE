Guide

Up and running in four steps

  1. Join

    Join the Discord. After the 72-hour trial, grab your free key there.

  2. Copy

    Copy the VALOR HUB loader from the script hub above.

  3. Paste

    Join a supported game and paste the loader into your executor.

  4. Run

    Execute it and the panel for that game opens.
