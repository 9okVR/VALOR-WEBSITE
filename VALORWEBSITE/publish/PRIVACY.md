# VALOR Privacy Policy

Oct 29, 2026

We collect only what we need to run VALOR HUB. We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers or use advertising trackers. This policy covers the VALOR website, VALOR HUB scripts and our Discord server.

## 1. No website accounts

The website has no accounts, sign-up forms or logins. You don't need to sign in to use VALOR HUB; you get access through the script and our Discord server.

We may add sign-in with Discord in the future, most likely only if we release the VALOR app. If we do, we'll update this policy before it goes live and list exactly what we request.

## 2. What the website stores and loads

- **No cookies.** The website sets no cookies of any kind, including sign-in cookies.
- **One value in local storage** remembers your choice in the cookie bar. It stays on your device and is never sent to us. The website has no analytics.
- **Google Fonts**, for typefaces. Your browser sends your IP address to Google to fetch them.
- **unpkg**, a public code library server, for the 3D background. It receives your IP address the same way.
- **Discord**, when you click a Discord link. Discord's own privacy policy applies there.

## 3. What VALOR HUB scripts send

The script's loader sends usage stats so we can fix what breaks and catch game updates and kick waves fast.

| What | Details | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your Roblox account | Username, display name, user ID, account age, Premium | Count players, run the 72-hour trial, show who is playing |
| Game and server | Game, place, server ID and size | See which panels are used; our staff can see which public server you're in |
| Executor and device | Executor name, phone or PC, which executor functions are missing | Find what breaks on which executor |
| How the panel runs | Switches that are on, the last ones you changed, errors and which feature caused them, time each feature takes, FPS and ping | Fix bugs and slow features |
| Kicks and disconnects | The message Roblox shows you, and your character's movement in the last 60 seconds (speed, sudden jumps, walk speed) | Find what gets players kicked |
| Game results | What a panel counts, for example Volleyball touches | See what each panel achieves |
| Problem reports | Your message, plus the details above | Fix the problem you reported |
| Country | The country you play from. Your IP address itself is never stored: it's turned into a scrambled code right away | See where players are from, and stop abuse of the free trial and our servers |

## 4. The VALOR app

The VALOR app isn't released, so it doesn't collect or send anything. If we ever release it, we'll update this policy first.

## 5. Payments

VALOR is free right now and takes no payments. When Premium launches, payments will go through a payment provider and we'll never see or store your full card details. We'll keep your Discord user ID and Premium status to give you Premium, plus the payment records the law requires, and this policy will be updated before then.

## 6. How long we keep it

Detailed script events are deleted after 30 days. A short record of each script run (account, game, time played, how it ended) stays on our servers with no set end date, so we can see trends over time. We may delete old records later, and we delete yours when you ask (section 9).

## 7. Who sees it

The VALOR team. It's stored on our servers, and daily summaries and alerts go to a private staff channel in our Discord. We share it only with the services needed to run VALOR HUB, such as hosting, or when the law requires it.

## 8. Children

You must be at least 13 years old to use VALOR or join the server, or 16 if you live in the European Union or European Economic Area. VALOR is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If we learn that a user is under 13, we block their access and delete their data.

## 9. Data requests

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the data we hold about you. Email okvr500@gmail.com or open a ticket in our [Discord](https://discord.gg/GSAHydBefQ), and include your Roblox username, and your Discord username if you have one, so we can find your records. Deleting removes your script records, except payment records we must keep by law. We usually reply within a few days, and always within 30 days.

## 10. On your device

The script saves your settings in your executor's VALOR\_HUB folder. If a run ends in a crash, its last stats are saved there too and sent the next time you run VALOR HUB.

## 11. Cookie policy

The website sets no cookies: no sign-in, tracking, analytics or advertising cookies. The only thing it stores is a local storage value that remembers your choice in the cookie bar. It stays on your device, and you can clear it in your browser settings.

The cookie bar lets you accept, keep only necessary cookies, or decline, and all three carry equal weight. Right now all three choices work the same way, because there is nothing optional to turn on. If we ever add cookies, including a sign-in cookie, we'll list them here first, and non-essential ones will load only after you accept.

## 12. Changes to this policy

This policy will change when Premium launches, and if we add website logins or release the VALOR app. Changes are announced in the Discord server; the date at the top shows the latest version.
