Privacy Policy

Oct 29, 2026

We collect only what we need to run VALOR HUB. We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers or use advertising trackers. This policy covers the VALOR website, VALOR HUB scripts and our Discord server.

1. No website accounts

The website has no accounts, sign-up forms or logins. You don't need to sign in to use VALOR HUB; you get access through the script and our Discord server.

We may add sign-in with Discord in the future, most likely only if we release the VALOR app. If we do, we'll update this policy before it goes live and list exactly what we request.

2. What the website stores and loads

  • No cookies. The website sets no cookies of any kind, including sign-in cookies.
  • One value in local storage remembers your choice in the cookie bar. It stays on your device and is never sent to us. The website has no analytics.
  • Google Fonts, for typefaces. Your browser sends your IP address to Google to fetch them.
  • unpkg, a public code library server, for the 3D background. It receives your IP address the same way.
  • Discord, when you click a Discord link. Discord's own privacy policy applies there.

11. Cookie policy

The website sets no cookies: no sign-in, tracking, analytics or advertising cookies. The only thing it stores is a local storage value that remembers your choice in the cookie bar. It stays on your device, and you can clear it in your browser settings.

The cookie bar lets you accept, keep only necessary cookies, or decline, and all three carry equal weight. Right now all three choices work the same way, because there is nothing optional to turn on. If we ever add cookies, including a sign-in cookie, we'll list them here first, and non-essential ones will load only after you accept.
