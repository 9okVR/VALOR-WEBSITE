How it works

You playA script with a database feature keeps a small summary: which bey you fought, how it played, whether it won. Never your name.
It adds upEvery hour, everyone's summaries are added together on our server.
Only what holds upA result shows once enough different players back it up, so one person can't fake it.
Everyone plays betterScripts read the results back and start from what already works.
Enough players first3 or more players on 3 or more networks before anything shows.
No one can flood itEach player's share is capped and the middle value is used.
Nothing personalOnly game results are kept. Nothing here says who played.
